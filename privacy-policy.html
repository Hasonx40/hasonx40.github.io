Chapter III: Target Hunter (Levels 51–75)

Locate the highlighted target pair first, then adapt immediately as the top and bottom card rows swap places mid-battle!

Official Support & FAQ (Destek Merkezi)

Need assistance with MindFlip: Card Kingdoms? Check our frequently asked questions below or contact the developer directly.

Q: How do I unlock Chapter 2 and Chapter 3?

Completing the first 7 levels of Chapter 1 (Levels 1–7) automatically unlocks Chapter 2. Completing the first 7 levels of Chapter 2 (Levels 26–32) unlocks Chapter 3.

Q: How does Nearby Bluetooth Duel work without an internet connection?

Open Multiplayer Arena ➔ Nearby Device (Bluetooth) on two iPhones or iPads. One player taps "Create Room (Host)" and the other taps "Join Room". Grant Local Network & Bluetooth permissions when prompted.

Q: How can I earn more "Find Pair" (Auto-Match) Jokers?

You earn +1 Auto-Match Joker automatically every time you achieve two consecutive 5-Star level ratings, or by tapping the "+1 Joker" rewarded video button on the game board.

Privacy Policy & Data Protection (Gizlilik Politikası)

Effective Date: November 10, 2026  |  Application: MindFlip: Card Kingdoms (iOS & iPadOS)

1. Overview & Commitment to Privacy

Hasan Orçun Özkaya ("Developer", "we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. MindFlip: Card Kingdoms is designed as a family-friendly brain-training game. We do not require account registration, and we do not collect, store, or sell personal identity information such as your real name, home address, phone number, or email address on any external server.

2. Local Data Storage

Your player profile nicknames, level progression, star ratings, and sound preferences are stored locally on your device using Apple's standard UserDefaults storage. Deleting the app removes this local data permanently.

3. Advertising, Google AdMob & App Tracking Transparency (ATT)

To keep MindFlip: Card Kingdoms free to play, we use Google AdMob (ca-app-pub-1191704940665935) to display banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded video advertisements.

  • On iOS/iPadOS 14.5+, our app requests your permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework before accessing your device's Advertising Identifier (IDFA).
  • If you select "Ask App Not to Track", Google AdMob will only serve non-personalized (contextual) advertisements and will not track your activity across other companies' apps or websites.
  • Our authorized digital sellers declaration is publicly verifiable at https://hasonx40.github.io/app-ads.txt.

4. Apple Game Center & Local Bluetooth Multiplayer

  • Apple Game Center: When you open the Global Leaderboard or Online Matchmaking, Apple's GameKit framework authenticates your Game Center nickname and submits your high score governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Bluetooth / Local Wi-Fi Duels: Nearby device connections use Apple's MultipeerConnectivity framework solely to synchronize card flips and scores between two consenting nearby devices. No chat, voice, or personal files are transmitted.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR)

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13. All gameplay features are safe for players of all ages.
